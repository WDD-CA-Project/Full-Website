This project connects directly to Sustainable Development Goal 15: Life on Land, which calls for the conservation, restoration, and sustainable use of forests. SDG 15 highlights the urgency of halting deforestation, protecting biodiversity, restoring degraded ecosystems, and promoting sustainable forest management. By understanding the causes and consequences of forest loss, we can support the global effort to preserve healthy terrestrial ecosystems.
