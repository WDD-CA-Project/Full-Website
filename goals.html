THE 17 GOALS

SUSTAINABILE DEVELOPMENT

What is Sustainability?

Sustainability means meeting the needs of today without compromising the ability of future generations to meet theirs. It balances environmental protection, social equity, and economic growth, ensuring that people and planet thrive together.

Life On Land

There are 17 Sustainable Development Goals set by the United Nations to build a better future. This website focuses on Goal 15: Life on Land, but there are many more goals addressing poverty, hunger, education, climate action, and beyond. Together, they form a roadmap for global sustainability.

🌱 Learn More About All Goals

The 17 Sustainable Development Goals

Goal 1: No Poverty

End poverty in all its forms everywhere.

Goal 2: Zero Hunger

Achieve food security and promote sustainable agriculture.

Goal 3: Good Health & Well-being

Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all ages.

Goal 4: Quality Education

Provide inclusive and equitable education for all.

Goal 5: Gender Equality

Achieve equality and empower women and girls.

Goal 6: Clean Water & Sanitation

Ensure availability of clean water and sanitation.

Goal 7: Affordable & Clean Energy

Ensure access to sustainable energy for all.

Goal 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth

Promote sustained, inclusive economic growth and decent work.

Goal 9: Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Build resilient infrastructure and foster innovation.

Goal 10: Reduced Inequalities

Reduce inequality within and among countries.

Goal 11: Sustainable Cities & Communities

Make cities inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable.

Goal 12: Responsible Consumption & Production

Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns.

Goal 13: Climate Action

Take urgent action to combat climate change.

Goal 14: Life Below Water

Conserve oceans, seas, and marine resources.

Goal 15: Life on Land

Protect, restore, and promote sustainable use of ecosystems.

Goal 16: Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions

Promote peaceful and inclusive societies.

Goal 17: Partnerships for the Goals

Strengthen global partnerships to achieve all goals.
