Land Degradation & Desertification

Land degradation is the decline in the land’s ability to support life, often caused by deforestation, overuse, and climate effects. SDG 15.3 aims to combat and reverse this by 2030.

Key Concepts

Land Degradation

The long-term reduction in soil health and productivity impacting ecosystems and agriculture.

Desertification

The degradation of drylands resulting from unsustainable use and climate impacts.

Why it Matters

It threatens food security, biodiversity, water resources, and livelihoods worldwide.

Causes

  • Deforestation and vegetation loss
  • Overgrazing and unsustainable farming
  • Urbanization and infrastructure growth
  • Climate change and drought

How You Can Help

  • Adopt sustainable farming and land practices
  • Support forest and wetland restoration projects
  • Learn more and spread awareness
