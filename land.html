Land Degradation & Desertification

What is land degradation?

When soil and land lose their ability to support plants and animals, usually because of human actions or climate.

What is desertification?

Dry places becoming even dryer and less useful for farming or wildlife due to bad land use and weather changes.

Why it is important

This causes less food, harm to plants and animals, and problems for people who live on the land.

How does it happen?

  • Cutting down too many trees
  • Using land too much for farming or raising animals
  • Building cities on fertile land
  • Less rain or more droughts

How to help

  • Farm and use land in a smarter way
  • Plant trees and save forests
  • Support projects that fix land
  • Learn and teach others
