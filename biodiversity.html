Causes and Impacts of Biodiversity Loss

    Causes:

  • Deforestation: destroys habitats and reduces forest cover.
  • Climate change: alters ecosystems and species survival.
  • Pollution: contaminates soil, water, and air.

    Impacts:

  • Species extinction: ecosystems lose balance and resilience.
  • Food insecurity: reduced crop yields and fisheries.
  • Loss of ecosystem services: less clean water, fertile soil, and carbon storage.

🌍Facts

FACT 1

Desertification impacts nearly 2 billion people worldwide, threatening food security, water access, and livelihoods.

Affects 25% of Earth's land surface

FACT 2

Every year, the world loses 24 billion tons of fertile soil due to unsustainable farming, deforestation, and climate change.

Soil loss reduces crop yields by up to 50%
