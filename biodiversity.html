Biodiversity & Wildlife

Biodiversity is the variety of all living things on Earth, from tiny bacteria to large animals and plants. It includes the different species in a habitat, the variety within those species (genetic diversity), and the different ecosystems like forests and oceans. Essentially, it's the variety of life at all its levels.

Species Biodiversity

Variety of species across the planet.

Genetic Biodiversity

Diversity of genes within species.

Ecosystem Biodiversity

Variety of habitats and ecosystems.

Causes of Biodiversity Loss

  • Deforestation: destroys habitats and reduces forest cover.
  • Climate change: alters ecosystems and species survival.
  • Pollution: contaminates soil, water, and air.

Impacts of Biodiversity Loss

  • Species extinction: ecosystems lose balance and resilience.
  • Food insecurity: reduced crop yields and fisheries.
  • Loss of ecosystem services: less clean water, fertile soil, and carbon storage.

Facts

Over 1 million species are at risk of extinction due to habitat loss, climate change, and human activity.

Rapid decline in global biodiversity

Major impact on forests and ecosystems

Solutions & Prevention

Agroforestry

Combines trees and crops to restore soil and biodiversity.

Sustainable Farming

Adopt eco-friendly practices that preserve soil and water.

Reforestation

Plant native trees and restore degraded ecosystems.

Protect Habitats

Establish reserves and safeguard forests, wetlands, and wildlife areas.
